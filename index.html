01 — THE BASICS

What is food preservation?

Food preservation means using methods that slow spoilage and help food remain safe and usable for longer.

❄️

Refrigeration

Keeps food cold to slow the growth of many microorganisms and slow chemical changes.

LOW TEMPERATURE
🫙

Canning

Uses suitable heat treatment and sealed containers to prevent contamination and control microbes.

HEAT + SEALING
💧

Drying

Removes moisture that many microorganisms need to grow.

LESS AVAILABLE WATER
KEY IDEA Preservation slows spoilage; it does not make every food last forever.
02 — THE PROBLEM

Why does food spoil?

Food changes over time because of living organisms and natural chemical processes.

01

Bacteria

Some bacteria multiply in food and may cause spoilage or foodborne illness.

02

Molds and yeasts

These fungi can grow on suitable foods, causing visible mold, odors, or fermentation.

03

Enzymes and oxidation

Natural enzymes and reactions with oxygen can change color, texture, flavor, and quality.

🔬
THE SCIENCE OF SPOILAGE
●✳○✣●

Warmth, moisture, time, and the type of food all affect how quickly spoilage occurs.

REMEMBER Food can contain harmful microorganisms even when it looks and smells normal.
03 — THE MAIN QUESTION

How does refrigeration work?

A refrigerator removes heat from its interior and releases that heat into the surrounding room.

🌡️
STEP 01

Heat is removed

The cooling system transfers heat out of the refrigerator.

→
❄️
STEP 02

Food stays cold

The refrigerator maintains a low temperature, ideally at or below 4°C.

→
🦠
STEP 03

Growth slows

Many microorganisms grow and reproduce more slowly in the cold.

04 — MICROBIOLOGY

Cold temperatures slow microbial growth.

Cold does not instantly kill all microorganisms. It mainly slows the growth of many of them.

❄️
0–4°C

06 — EVERYDAY APPLICATION

Refrigeration and food safety.

Proper storage is just as important as having a refrigerator.

✓

Check the temperature

Keep the fridge at 4°C (40°F) or below.

✓

Store food promptly

Refrigerate perishable food within 2 hours, or within 1 hour when the temperature is above 32°C (90°F).

✓

Cover and separate

Use clean, covered containers and keep raw meat separate from ready-to-eat food.

✓

Follow storage guidance

Observe food labels and recommended storage times. A fridge does not make food safe indefinitely.

FOOD SAFETY Never rely on smell or appearance alone to decide whether food is safe.
07 — EVALUATION

Benefits and limitations.

Refrigeration is useful, but it is not a complete solution to every food safety problem.

＋ Benefits

  • Slows the growth of many microorganisms.
  • Helps maintain food quality and freshness.
  • Reduces food waste when used correctly.
  • Helps preserve many perishable foods.

− Limitations

  • Some microorganisms can still grow in the fridge.
  • Cold does not reliably destroy pathogens.
  • Food quality still changes over time.
  • Power failures and poor handling can affect safety.
SCIENCE CONCLUSIONCold slows many processes, but proper handling still matters.
